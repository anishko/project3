title: Applying for FEMA Disaster Assistance
source_name: DisasterAssistance.gov (FEMA) – team summary
source_url: https://www.disasterassistance.gov/
category: general
---
After a federally declared disaster, people in the declared area may be able to get FEMA Individual Assistance for needs such as temporary housing, basic home repairs and other serious disaster-related expenses not covered by insurance.

How to apply: online at DisasterAssistance.gov, through the FEMA mobile app, by calling the FEMA helpline, or in person at a Disaster Recovery Center. Check the FEMA website or the declaration for the current phone number, center locations and the application deadline.

Have ready: your Social Security number, current contact information and the address of the damaged home, a description of the damage and losses, insurance information, household income, and bank account information if you want direct deposit.

File an insurance claim first if you have insurance. FEMA cannot pay for losses that insurance already covers, but you can still apply and send FEMA your insurance settlement or denial letter later.

After applying, FEMA may schedule a home inspection. Keep receipts for repairs and expenses and take photos of damage. If FEMA's decision letter says you are ineligible, read the reason carefully; often a missing document is the issue, and you have the right to appeal within the time limit stated in the letter.

Beware of scams. FEMA staff carry official identification and never charge a fee to apply or for an inspection.
